# Tokenization and Text Preprocessing (NLP)

Tokenization is the process of breaking text into smaller units called
tokens, such as words, subwords, or characters. It is one of the first
and most important preprocessing steps in Natural Language Processing
because machine learning models cannot directly understand raw text.

Text preprocessing also includes converting text to lowercase, removing
punctuation, eliminating stop words, handling emojis or special
characters, and applying stemming or lemmatization to reduce words to
their root form. These steps help reduce noise and improve model
performance.

Modern NLP models often use subword tokenization techniques like Byte
Pair Encoding (BPE) or WordPiece, which handle rare or unseen words more
effectively than traditional word-level tokenization. Proper
tokenization improves language understanding and enables applications
such as chatbots, machine translation, text summarization, sentiment
analysis, and question-answering systems.
