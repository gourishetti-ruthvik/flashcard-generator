# Word Embeddings in NLP

Word embeddings are numerical vector representations of words that
capture their semantic meaning and relationships. Instead of
representing words as isolated symbols, embeddings place similar words
close together in a high-dimensional vector space. This allows NLP
models to understand context and similarity between words.

Traditional embedding methods include Word2Vec, GloVe, and FastText.
These models learn word representations by analyzing large text corpora
and identifying patterns in word co-occurrences. Modern
transformer-based models such as BERT generate contextual embeddings,
meaning the representation of a word changes depending on the sentence
in which it appears.

Word embeddings are essential for many NLP tasks, including sentiment
analysis, document classification, named entity recognition, semantic
search, recommendation systems, and conversational AI. They provide
meaningful input features that help machine learning models process
natural language more accurately and efficiently.
